# Evacuation Announcement Response

You're at a sold-out indoor concert. Read the short scenario, then answer every
question in the questionnaire as **you** would actually behave — not as you think
people *should* behave. There are no right answers; honest hesitation, skepticism
or stubbornness is exactly what we need to know.

## How to answer

- Read the scenario before you start.
- Answer every required question.
- For multiple-choice, use the listed option ids.
- For rating scales, use a whole number in the given range.
- Add a short reason and your confidence (0–1) where you can.
