# Scenario — concert hall evacuation

**Venue:** An indoor concert hall, about the size of a basketball court, standing
room only. Roughly 200 people.

**You came in** through the large main entrance on the west side, like almost
everyone else. The stage is on the east side; most people are packed near it,
including you.

**Exits:** Besides the main entrance there are three smaller exits you may or may
not have noticed: one next to the stage (east), one on the south wall and one on
the north wall. All are marked with green exit signs.

**What happens:** In the middle of a song the music stops, the house lights come
on and a fire alarm starts ringing. You do not see smoke or flames.

Over the next minute, staff may make announcements over loudspeakers that are
mounted next to each exit — you only hear the speaker near you. Announcements can
sound calm, urgent, or give a reason (for example, that one exit is congested).
